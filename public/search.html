Fluffy Pancakes

Category: Breakfast

Cooking Time: 20 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Enjoyment Rating: ★★★★★

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk

Instructions

  1. Combine the dry ingredients.
  2. Mix in the egg and milk.
  3. Heat a lightly greased pan.
  4. Pour batter into the pan.
  5. Cook until golden on both sides.
